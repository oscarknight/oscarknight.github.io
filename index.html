Starting a small blog

Sample post · Why keep a public notebook?

A short note can capture what a finished project leaves out. What did I try first? Which assumption held up? What would I do differently next time? Those are the details worth coming back to.

A photo and a few sentences can be enough. The goal is to write while the details are still fresh.

Sketch the boundary first

Sample post · A thermodynamics study note

Before writing an energy balance, draw a line around the part of the problem you want to study. For a turbine, that might be a boundary that encloses the casing and cuts across the inlet and outlet.

Then mark what crosses the line. The mass streams carry energy, the shaft can deliver work, and heat can cross the casing. That sketch gives each term in the balance a physical meaning.
